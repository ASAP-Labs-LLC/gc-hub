**v5.0.0: the redesigned Samples page and Analysis, the address bar as the link, and "Select all matching this filter".** This is a MAJOR release because the main page changes how results are displayed. No D2887/D86 number is calculated, stored or exported differently. The results CSV LEM reads, Export to LIMS and the report PDFs are unchanged.

## Upgrading from v4.0.0

- Install as usual with Settings > "Restart & install v5.0.0". Restart waits for any running import or other background work.
- **No database change.** v5 reads the same data as v4.0.0, so rolling back to v4.0.0 is safe.
- **The old main page stays at `/classic`** for this release, in case something you rely on isn't in the new page yet. Old links (`/?sample=…`, `/samples/<id>` from v4, `/lab/<id>`) open in the new page.
- Open pages ask to reload after the update. Everyone keeps their sign-in.
- No agent reinstall; the hub tray updates with the release.

## Samples

The home page is the new **Samples** page, built like the instrument pages: the sidebar, a list on the left and the sample on the right.

- **Where it is:** `/` and `/samples`. One run is `/samples/<id>`, with **Overview**, **Compare** and **Data** at `/samples/<id>`, `/samples/<id>/compare` and `/samples/<id>/data`.
- **The address bar is the link.** Whatever you are looking at is in the address: the sample, the view, the Compare standard (`?standard=`), and the list's filters, search and order (`?instrument=gc2&status=held&q=403&sort=lab&notsent=1`). Back and Forward step through what you looked at, and reloading or pasting the address opens the same view. Over the lab network the address is the local one (`http://asapsv1:5560/…`); **Copy link** still copies the gc.asaplabs.net form, which opens from anywhere.
- **The list:**
  - chips for the instrument and the status (Held, Error, Processing, Final), plus **Not sent to QBench** (final results never uploaded). The Held and Error chips carry their counts, so "Held · 3" is both the number and the filter;
  - **Filter lab ID** (Ctrl K jumps to it), day headings (Today, Yesterday, the date), and the Newest / Lab ID order, remembered per browser;
  - full lab IDs, run numbers (`40304 (2)`), the instrument, the time, and the best fit or method;
  - every held or failed row says why and links to the fix: **Calibration**, **Corrections**, **Methods**, **Release backfill**, or a **Re-process** button;
  - rows update by themselves as runs arrive and change.
- **Selecting many:** hover a row to see its checkbox. Click it, shift-click for a range, drag down the checkboxes, or tick the box above the list for every row shown. Ctrl-click (⌘-click) a row also adds it. A bar at the bottom offers **Re-process**, **Export to LIMS**, **Add to report queue** and **Download reports**.
- **Select all N matching this filter.** When every row shown is ticked and more samples match the filter than are loaded, the bar offers "Select all 1,234 matching this filter", as Gmail does. It uses the hub's own filter, not the loaded page. The action then asks first, naming the count and the filter, runs in steps with progress and a **Stop**, and ends with one line saying what happened ("Re-process: 1,230 of 1,234 done · 4 refused"). **Clear selection** undoes it.
- **The sample header:** the lab ID, a status pill with its reason (and the fix), the instrument, the injection time, the method and revision, and the backfill and corrected-time marks. **Export report**, **Add to queue** and "⋯": **Re-process**, **Export to LIMS**, **Download CDF** and **Copy link**.
- **Overview:** the chromatogram (drag to zoom, double-click to reset; carbon marks from the run's own calibration), the Results card (Recovery | D86 | D2887, **D86 corrected** on or off (the same choice as on Results and Settings; off until you turn it on), 40%/60% midpoints explained on hover, best fit and flags), and the lab ID's other runs.
- **Imported (v1) results** show their stored numbers on Overview and Data too, although the hub has no CDF or curve for them.
- **Data:** every number to two decimals (D2887, D86 before and after the correction, the correction itself, and **Copy table**), the calibration used (file and anchors), the blank used, the method, the CDF, when it was sent to QBench, and the revision history in plain words.
- **Keyboard:** ↑ ↓ or j k step through the list, Shift adds to the selection, x or Space ticks the open sample, Esc clears the selection, Ctrl K filters.
- **Themes:** the page follows System, Light and Dark, the chart included.

**Old links keep working.** `/lab/<lab ID>` (what COA Reviewer links to) opens its newest run on the new page. `/samples/<id>` links open the new page. `/?sample=<id>` is rewritten to `/samples/<id>`, and the old `/?open=settings` and `/?open=help` open the Settings and Help pages.

**The classic page stays for this release at `/classic`**, with its own links at `/classic/lab/<lab ID>` and `/classic/samples/<id>[/compare|/data]`. It will be removed in the next release.

**Small additions to the hub (read-only):** `GET /api/files/ids` (the ids matching the list's filters, at most 20,000, for "Select all matching"), `GET /api/samples/<id>/cdf` (Download CDF), and `notsent=1` plus `qbench_uploaded_at` on `/api/files`.

## Backfill selection and themes

On an instrument's Backfill section you can now Select all, shift-click a range, or drag down the boxes (mouse or touch). Space and Shift+Space work too.
The bar above the list says "N selected · Release N". Large releases go 500 runs at a time and show their progress.
The selection stays put while the page updates itself. Runs released elsewhere drop out of it.
Each run says why it is backfill, e.g. "injected 14:02 · before GC-2 went live (Sep 30 14:30)".
Theme is now System, Light or Dark (user menu). System is the default and follows the computer's setting as it changes. The new Samples page follows it too, its chart included (the classic page at /classic keeps its dark look).
None of this changes a result, report, CSV or export.

## Compare and the report queue

The Analysis tab is redesigned as **Compare**, one of a sample's three views (Overview · Compare · Data). The findings, the conclusion and every report number come from the hub exactly as before: the same analysis pass builds the page, the PDF and the QBench upload, and the report payloads are unchanged.

- **The standard is picked for you.** Compare opens with the best-fitting comparison standard ("Best fit is Diesel ULSD Std (0.94)" beside the picker). Pick another and that choice is remembered for this sample on this computer. The address bar keeps it (`/samples/<id>/compare?standard=…`), so a sent link opens on the same standard.
- **Two graphs, in the new monochrome style.** The Trend shows the sample as a dark line over the standard as a light area, with the ranges shaded and the sample's own carbon numbers along the top. The Difference shows where the sample is higher (red fill) or lower (blue fill), the counted sharp peaks and the threshold lines. Drag to zoom (both graphs follow), double-click to reset, and open either full screen.
- **Findings are a list.** Each range that deviates is a row with its size and direction ("Moderate · higher"), in the hub's own words; **Show** zooms the graphs to it. The 3-line report box is gone.
- **No Run Analysis button.** Changing anything recomputes straight away.
- **Adjust** opens a drawer beside the graphs, and the sample list steps aside while it is open. It holds the trend line (baseline, detail, smoothing), the deviation thresholds, the ranges and "Show up to (min)". A value that can't be used is marked and nothing is recomputed until it is fixed (for example, a moderate threshold below the marginal one, or a range that ends before it starts). **Save as default** asks for the admin password once, as elsewhere.
- **The conclusion is read-only until you click Edit.** An edited conclusion says so, and **Use the generated text** puts the hub's back. Reports and the queue use your text.
- **Comments and annotations** are as before: preset chips, a comment box, and **Annotate** (drag across the Trend to mark a region). **Clear annotations** has moved into the Annotate menu, away from the tool itself, and still asks first, naming the count.
- **Export report** downloads one PDF built with what Compare shows: the standard, the parameters and ranges, and your conclusion. The sheet lists those settings before you download.
- **Add to queue** (it replaces the red "Send to Queue" and the QBench export button) adds the sample's report to the **report queue** and says which standard it was compared with. The top bar shows **Report queue N** on every page while the queue holds reports. The queue lasts until the browser tab is closed.
- **The report queue sheet**: every queued report with its standard; **Remove**, **Clear**; **Download all** (one PDF, or a ZIP the hub builds in the background, with its progress; it also shows in "Running now"); and **Upload to QBench**: sign in (the saved QBench sign-in is filled in), then progress per sample, **Skip** for one sample, **Stop** for all, and, when QBench refuses the sign-in partway through, a box for the password again, after which the upload carries on. Closing the sheet or opening another page does not stop an upload; the button shows "Uploading 3/8" meanwhile. Reports already sent are marked and are not sent twice.
- **Comparison Export** is not offered in the new design (Ryan does not use it).

## Results, Settings and Help

**Results** (sidebar, `/results`) shows every run's D2887 and D86 temperatures side by side, under one "D2887 (°C)" and one "D86 (°C)" heading. The headings come from the hub's own column list, so each one sits over its own numbers (the old Distillation Data tab once had them swapped).
- **Key points** (IBP, 10 %, 50 %, 90 %, FBP) by default; **All points** shows every cut, the fit score and the source file. The choice is remembered on this computer.
- Filter by lab ID, GC, time (today, last 7/30/90 days), status (final, held, error, processing) and Flagged. The filters are in the address, so a filtered view can be bookmarked or sent, and Back and Forward work.
- Each run shows its status as a mark and, when it is not final, why. Backfill runs are labelled. Numbers are right-aligned with one decimal; hover for two.
- **Corrected D86** works as before: on, the numbers written to the results file (with the GC's correction factors); off, the conversion before them. 40 % and 60 % are midpoints and say so when you hover them.
- A run's lab ID opens its data view. Tick up to six runs to draw their distillation curves on one chart (drag to zoom, double-click to reset).
- **Download CSV** saves the runs the filters show, with every cut, as shown (corrected or not).

**Settings** (user menu, `/settings`) replaces the old Settings window, grouped by who changes what:
- *This browser*: the theme, and whether Results shows D86 with correction factors first.
- *Sample flags*: the flag rules, for anyone.
- *Best fit*, *Findings*, *Compare defaults*, *Comparison standards* (tag with a GC, rename, delete, add from a run's lab ID) and the *LEM address*: these ask for the admin password once, in the masked dialog, and keep it in the tab for 15 minutes. No more password boxes that show what you type.
- *QBench*: the API key (admin; checked with QBench before it is saved) and which web login uploads use.
- *Server*: where the hub keeps things, read-only and folded away. The old watch folder, processed-CDF folder and results CSV fields (unused since v2) are gone.

**Restart** moved to Admin · Server, with the same "Restart & install vX" when an update is staged and the same warning when work would be cut short.

**Help** (user menu, `/help`) is one short page in plain words, brought up to date (per-GC correction factors, the hub, the marks).

**Notifications**: the bell opens a panel listing what needs a person, newest first, each with its level in words, when it came, and a link to where it is fixed. Dismiss one or all. New ones appear without a refresh.

None of this changes a result, report, CSV or export.
