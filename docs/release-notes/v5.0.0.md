## Compare and the report queue

The Analysis tab is redesigned as **Compare**, one of a sample's three views (Overview · Compare · Data). The findings, the conclusion and every report number come from the hub exactly as before: the same analysis pass builds the page, the PDF and the QBench upload, and the report payloads are unchanged.

- **The standard is picked for you.** Compare opens with the best-fitting comparison standard ("Best fit is Diesel ULSD Std (0.94)" beside the picker). Pick another and that choice is remembered for this sample on this computer. The address bar keeps it (`/samples/<id>/compare?standard=…`), so a sent link opens on the same standard.
- **Two graphs, in the new monochrome style.** The Trend shows the sample as a dark line over the standard as a light area, with the ranges shaded and the sample's own carbon numbers along the top. The Difference shows where the sample is higher (red fill) or lower (blue fill), the counted sharp peaks and the threshold lines. Drag to zoom (both graphs follow), double-click to reset, and open either full screen.
- **Findings are a list.** Each range that deviates is a row with its size and direction ("Moderate · higher"), in the hub's own words; **Show** zooms the graphs to it. The 3-line report box is gone.
- **No Run Analysis button.** Changing anything recomputes straight away.
- **Adjust** opens a drawer beside the graphs, and the sample list steps aside while it is open. It holds the trend line (baseline, detail, smoothing), the deviation thresholds, the ranges and "Show up to (min)". A value that can't be used is marked and nothing is recomputed until it is fixed (for example, a moderate threshold below the marginal one, or a range that ends before it starts). **Save as default** asks for the admin password once, as elsewhere.
- **The conclusion is read-only until you click Edit.** An edited conclusion says so, and **Use the generated text** puts the hub's back. Reports and the queue use your text.
- **Comments and annotations** are as before: preset chips, a comment box, and **Annotate** (drag across the Trend to mark a region). **Clear annotations** has moved into the Annotate menu, away from the tool itself, and still asks first, naming the count.
- **Export report** downloads one PDF built with what Compare shows: the standard, the parameters and ranges, and your conclusion. The sheet lists those settings before you download.
- **Add to queue** (it replaces the red "Send to Queue" and the QBench export button) adds the sample's report to the **report queue** and says which standard it was compared with. The top bar shows **Report queue N** on every page while the queue holds reports. The queue lasts until the browser tab is closed.
- **The report queue sheet**: every queued report with its standard; **Remove**, **Clear**; **Download all** (one PDF, or a ZIP the hub builds in the background, with its progress; it also shows in "Running now"); and **Upload to QBench**: sign in (the saved QBench sign-in is filled in), then progress per sample, **Skip** for one sample, **Stop** for all, and, when QBench refuses the sign-in partway through, a box for the password again, after which the upload carries on. Closing the sheet or opening another page does not stop an upload; the button shows "Uploading 3/8" meanwhile. Reports already sent are marked and are not sent twice.
- **Comparison Export** is not offered in the new design (Ryan does not use it).
