**v5.0.0: the redesigned Samples page and Analysis, the address bar as the link, and "Select all matching this filter".** This is a MAJOR release because the main page changes how results are displayed. No D2887/D86 number is calculated, stored or exported differently. The results CSV LEM reads, Export to LIMS and the report PDFs are unchanged.

## Samples

The home page is the new **Samples** page, built like the instrument pages: the sidebar, a list on the left and the sample on the right.

- **Where it is:** `/` and `/samples`. One run is `/samples/<id>`, with **Overview**, **Compare** and **Data** at `/samples/<id>`, `/samples/<id>/compare` and `/samples/<id>/data`.
- **The address bar is the link.** Whatever you are looking at is in the address: the sample, the view, the Compare standard (`?standard=`), and the list's filters, search and order (`?instrument=gc2&status=held&q=403&sort=lab&notsent=1`). Back and Forward step through what you looked at, and reloading or pasting the address opens the same view. Over the lab network the address is the local one (`http://asapsv1:5560/…`); **Copy link** still copies the gc.asaplabs.net form, which opens from anywhere.
- **The list:**
  - chips for the instrument and the status (Held, Error, Processing, Final), plus **Not sent to QBench** (final results never uploaded). The Held and Error chips carry their counts, so "Held · 3" is both the number and the filter;
  - **Filter lab ID** (Ctrl K jumps to it), day headings (Today, Yesterday, the date), and the Newest / Lab ID order, remembered per browser;
  - full lab IDs, run numbers (`40304 (2)`), the instrument, the time, and the best fit or method;
  - every held or failed row says why and links to the fix: **Calibration**, **Corrections**, **Methods**, **Release backfill**, or a **Re-process** button;
  - rows update by themselves as runs arrive and change.
- **Selecting many:** hover a row to see its checkbox. Click it, shift-click for a range, drag down the checkboxes, or tick the box above the list for every row shown. Ctrl-click (⌘-click) a row also adds it. A bar at the bottom offers **Re-process**, **Export to LIMS**, **Add to report queue** and **Download reports**.
- **Select all N matching this filter.** When every row shown is ticked and more samples match the filter than are loaded, the bar offers "Select all 1,234 matching this filter", as Gmail does. It uses the hub's own filter, not the loaded page. The action then asks first, naming the count and the filter, runs in steps with progress and a **Stop**, and ends with one line saying what happened ("Re-process: 1,230 of 1,234 done · 4 refused"). **Clear selection** undoes it.
- **The sample header:** the lab ID, a status pill with its reason (and the fix), the instrument, the injection time, the method and revision, and the backfill and corrected-time marks. **Export report**, **Add to queue** and "⋯": **Re-process**, **Export to LIMS**, **Download CDF** and **Copy link**.
- **Overview:** the chromatogram (drag to zoom, double-click to reset; carbon marks from the run's own calibration), the Results card (Recovery | D86 | D2887, **D86 corrected** on or off and remembered, 40%/60% midpoints explained on hover, best fit and flags), and the lab ID's other runs.
- **Data:** every number to two decimals (D2887, D86 before and after the correction, the correction itself, and **Copy table**), the calibration used (file and anchors), the blank used, the method, the CDF, when it was sent to QBench, and the revision history in plain words.
- **Keyboard:** ↑ ↓ or j k step through the list, Shift adds to the selection, x or Space ticks the open sample, Esc clears the selection, Ctrl K filters.
- **Themes:** the page follows System, Light and Dark, the chart included.

**Old links keep working.** `/lab/<lab ID>` (what COA Reviewer links to) opens its newest run on the new page. `/samples/<id>` links open the new page. `/?sample=<id>` is rewritten to `/samples/<id>`.

**The classic page stays for this release at `/classic`**, with its own links at `/classic/lab/<lab ID>` and `/classic/samples/<id>[/compare|/data]`. It will be removed in the next release.

**Small additions to the hub (read-only):** `GET /api/files/ids` (the ids matching the list's filters, at most 20,000, for "Select all matching"), `GET /api/samples/<id>/cdf` (Download CDF), and `notsent=1` plus `qbench_uploaded_at` on `/api/files`.

## Backfill selection and themes

On an instrument's Backfill section you can now Select all, shift-click a range, or drag down the boxes (mouse or touch). Space and Shift+Space work too.
The bar above the list says "N selected · Release N". Large releases go 500 runs at a time and show their progress.
The selection stays put while the page updates itself. Runs released elsewhere drop out of it.
Each run says why it is backfill, e.g. "injected 14:02 · before GC-2 went live (Sep 30 14:30)".
Theme is now System, Light or Dark (user menu). System is the default and follows the computer's setting as it changes. The new Samples page follows it too, its chart included (the classic page at /classic keeps its dark look).
None of this changes a result, report, CSV or export.
