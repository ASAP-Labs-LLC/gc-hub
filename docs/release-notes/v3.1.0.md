## Sign-in and gc.asaplabs.net

**v3.1.0: everyone signs in, and https://gc.asaplabs.net is the hub's address.**
Until now the hub had no login: anyone who could reach it, on the lab network
or through https://gc.asaplabs.net, could read results, settings and
notifications, and through the tunnel every browser action failed with 403.
From this release:

- **Sign in with your LabLink account**, as in COA Reviewer: tap your card,
  or type your LabLink username and password. The hub asks LabVision
  (LabCore); it never stores your password. Everyone signs in once after the
  update; a session lasts until you sign out, 12 hours without use, or 14 days
  (7 days when you signed in through gc.asaplabs.net). The same rule applies on
  the lab network (`http://ASAPSV1:5560`).
- **"Signed in as <name> · Sign out"** is next to the version number at the
  bottom right of every page.
- **If LabVision can't be reached**, the sign-in page says so. On the lab
  network, "Sign in with the admin password" still works (the session is
  recorded as "Admin (break-glass)"); through gc.asaplabs.net only LabLink
  sign-in is offered. Changing the admin password signs every break-glass
  session out.
- **Comments are signed with your name.** The "Your initials" box is gone:
  the Analysis tab says "Commenting as <name>", and reports keep printing the
  initials, now taken from your account name (Ryan C → RC).
- **Who did what** is recorded by name and address in `app.log` and in every
  "by" field: reprocess, Export to LIMS, restart, QBench upload, Settings and
  admin changes, releases, conflict decisions, diagnostics.
- **Admin actions still need the admin password**, on top of signing in.
- **gc.asaplabs.net works fully**: saving, exporting, uploading and admin
  actions no longer fail with 403 through the tunnel. Plain `http://` through
  Cloudflare is redirected to `https://`.
- **Hub admin** has two new panels: **Hub address** (the address agents and
  the hub tray use; empty = https://gc.asaplabs.net) and **Signed-in
  sessions** (who is signed in, with Revoke and "Revoke all for this name").
- **Agent installers point at https://gc.asaplabs.net** unless an admin set
  another hub address; the "set the hub URL first" step is gone. The agent
  falls back to the hub's lab-network address when gc.asaplabs.net can't be
  reached, and says "Cloudflare blocked the agent: add the WAF skip rule in
  DEPLOY.md" if Cloudflare challenges it.
- **The hub tray's "Open in browser"** opens the hub address. Its pause, stop
  and restart controls still work only on ASAPSV1 itself, never through the
  tunnel.

**Before installing:** read DEPLOY.md "Sign-in and gc.asaplabs.net (v3.1)":
the Cloudflare settings (Always Use HTTPS, the WAF skip rule for the agents)
must be in place, and **a rollback to a release before v3.1.0 makes the hub
public again with no login**: pause the gc.asaplabs.net hostname in
Cloudflare first.

**Nothing about results changes**: no D2887/D86 number, the results CSV,
reports' content (apart from comment initials coming from the account name)
or Export to LIMS. The database gains a sessions table and three name
columns (schema v3); older releases still start on it.
