**v8.2.0: open a queued report as it was built, and a Learn page with short notes and walk-throughs.**

## Upgrading from v8.1.1

- Install as usual with Admin · Server > "Restart & install v8.2.0". No database change; rolling back is safe.
- Results, reports and what is uploaded to QBench don't change.

## New

- **Click a report in the queue to reopen it.** Each report's lab ID in the report queue is now a link. It opens the sample in Compare as the report was built:
  - its standard,
  - its trend settings and thresholds,
  - its "Show up to" limit,
  - its ranges,
  - its edited conclusion.

  The sample's annotations are there as always. It works from any page. On the Samples page the list keeps its filters. A report with no comparison opens the sample's Overview.
- **Learn** (in the sidebar, and linked from Help). Short notes for reading the GC, in four parts:
  - **Reading a chromatogram:** left is light and right is heavy; the carbon numbers along the top; the hump and the picket fence; shape, not height; the Trend and Difference charts; the ranges; and why a GC result is screening, not proof.
  - **The fuels we test:** Diesel #2 and #1, renewable diesel (R99, R100, HVO), biodiesel B100 and the B5/B20 blends, gasoline, and jet fuel/kerosene. Each has a sketch of its shape, its carbon range and its key limits.
  - **What we see most:** gasoline in diesel, low flash without gasoline, high flash, fuel mixes, a heavy end, biodiesel in diesel, and tank bottoms. Each says what it looks like on the GC, why it matters and how often we see it, from the lab's past runs and flash results.
  - **How to:** short walk-throughs that play by themselves. Pause, step through or jump to a step. They cover finding a sample, checking it in Compare, queueing reports, downloading a report and uploading to QBench.

## Fixes

- **A link to a sample's Compare view now opens on the standard in the link.** A link like `/samples/<id>/compare?standard=B` (a bookmark, or an address copied from the address bar) used to open a different standard if you had picked one for that sample earlier in the same browser.
- **Bulk reports use each run's best-fit standard.** On the Samples list, "Add to report queue" and "Download reports" for selected runs (and a report made while Compare isn't open) used the first standard in the list instead of the run's best fit. Reports added from Compare, or from the Export report sheet, were not affected.
