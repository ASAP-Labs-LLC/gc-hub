**v8.3.0: Patterns in Compare, a chromatogram lesson, the cases on one chart, and dark-mode walk-throughs.**

## Upgrading from v8.2.0

- Install as usual with Admin · Server > "Restart & install v8.3.0". No database change; rolling back is safe.
- Results, reports and what is uploaded to QBench don't change.

## New

- **Patterns in Compare.** Beside the findings, a short list of the cases we see most:
  - gasoline in diesel,
  - lighter fuel in diesel,
  - light ends missing,
  - renewable mixed in,
  - a heavy end,
  - biodiesel,
  - water.

  Hover over one to see what it would look like against the standard on screen, drawn on the Trend chart as a dashed line next to the standard and your sample. Click to keep it shown; click again to take it off. Each case has a one-line "what you see / what to check". The case is drawn from the standard you are comparing against, placed with the run's own calibration, so it lines up with the chart. It is a sketch to compare shapes, not a result.
- **Reading a chromatogram is a lesson.** On Learn, eleven short steps take you through one chart:
  - time runs left to right,
  - carbon numbers are on top,
  - skip the solvent,
  - the hump,
  - the picket fence,
  - the light end,
  - the Bio band,
  - the heavy end,
  - compare shapes, not heights,
  - the difference,
  - decide, then confirm.

  Each step marks its part of the chart. Go at your own pace with Back, Next or the dots. How to starts with it.
- **What we see most is one chart.** Diesel #2 is the reference, with every case drawn over it. Pick a case to see it bold, with its range and its difference from #2 (red more, blue less), and what to check beside it.
- **Water is a flat curve.** A sample that is mostly water gives a flat, low trace and a distillation that comes out wrong. The note says to check the water content (and water and sediment) first.
- **The walk-throughs follow the theme.** In dark mode they show dark screenshots, and switching the theme switches them. The highlight is now amber in light mode and yellow in dark, so it shows on both. Before, it was white in dark mode.
